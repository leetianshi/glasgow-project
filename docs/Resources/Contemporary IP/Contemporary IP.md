<span style="color:#B00020;">**[Chunck 010101]**</span><br>
**Key words**: copyright weaponization; noncopyright interests; market exclusivity; economic incentives; legal strategy<br>
**Essay-ready point**: Copyright weaponization occurs when copyright is invoked not to protect market exclusivity or economic incentives, but to advance noncopyright interests such as suppression, retaliation, reputation management, or privacy protection.<br>
**Original Sentence**: All these stories have one thing in common: they all involve copyright owners using copyright to advance noncopyright interests. This Article refers to these actions as ‘weaponizing copyright.’<br>

**Original Sentence**: Copyright grants authors exclusive rights in their works in order to encourage creation and dissemination of socially valuable works.<br>
**Page**: 195

<span style="color:#B00020;">**[Chunck 010103]**</span><br>
